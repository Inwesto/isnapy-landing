Who handles your information

Blue Surface Technologies Ltd. (BST), a private company registered in the Astana International Financial Centre in Kazakhstan, operates iSnapy and determines how it handles personal data. Write to the registered office below or email hello@isnapy.com. You can contact us without an iSnapy account.

This notice covers the 18+ pilot app, the public website, the video arena, support and reports. You may appear in somebody else’s clip or report even if you have never created an account. Read the section on your rights if that applies to you.

What we process and why

The table explains the main purposes and the legal bases we rely on where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. Accepting the Terms acknowledges this notice. That acceptance does not give blanket consent to optional processing.

What other people can see

Your public handle, avatar, bio, posts, clips, comments, social counts, scores and game activity can be visible to other users and guests. The public website can display clips from the public feed. A video may reveal voices, faces, backgrounds, locations or other identifying details. Viewers may record, save or reshare public material beyond iSnapy. Email addresses used for recovery and passwords are not public profile fields.

Use the device picker to choose media you want to upload. Where your device asks for permission, you can change it in system settings. Report publication of your personal details, intimate imagery or a child’s information through our safety contact. Identify the item without redistributing harmful files.

Ranking, automation and AI

Explore combines fresh clips with clips ranked using battle power, comments and likes. Im also mixes recent and established clips. The public arena can randomly select from this pool. Content metadata, rules and gameplay events produce character descriptions and scores. A keyword can trigger a review flag or affect a score. It cannot reliably determine what happened in a video or whether the depicted person is dangerous.

When optional measurement is on, iSnapy can calculate an experimental feed order in the background using language and region signals, follows and interactions. In this pilot the experiment does not replace the displayed order. We do not use the game scores to identify faces, diagnose emotions, decide access to credit or employment, or make a solely automated decision with comparable significant effects about a person. If a restriction seems mistaken, request human review. The pilot content licence does not authorize broad third-party AI training.

Service providers and international handling

Who receives data. DigitalOcean hosts the pilot API and media. GitHub Pages serves the public site and receives website request information, including IP addresses for security. The domain may use network or security providers to route traffic. The November backend uses Resend when configured to deliver verification and recovery mail. Apple and Expo may process device, installation, build or crash information under their respective roles when you use TestFlight or an internal development build. Mail providers handle messages you choose to send. Recipients also include the public for public content, authorized support personnel, professional advisers and competent authorities when necessary and lawful.

Countries and safeguards. BST operates from Kazakhstan. Hosting, email, website and distribution providers may process information in the EEA, the United States and other locations used for service delivery or support. Kazakhstan does not have an EU adequacy decision. Where the GDPR’s international-transfer rules apply, a transfer must have a valid route, such as an applicable adequacy decision covering the recipient or appropriate safeguards, ordinarily the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses with any necessary supplementary measures. Write to us for the relevant destinations, mechanism and a copy or description of safeguards, subject to lawful redactions. Hosting in one region alone does not determine where support or administration can access data.

Storage on your device and website requests

The app uses device storage for authentication and security. In the updated pilot, optional usage measurement starts off and uses a temporary session identifier when enabled. Earlier pilot versions could store a pseudonymous analytics identifier on the installation. Uninstalling may affect locally stored data according to the operating system. It does not delete server data.

These public legal pages use standard web requests to deliver the page and stylesheet. They contain no advertising pixels or analytics scripts. Our arena asks for an adult confirmation before it requests public clips. We do not set an advertising cookie on these pages. Infrastructure providers may process technical information for delivery and security. A third-party site or email app you choose to open has its own practices.

Retention and erasure

We keep account information while needed for the account. Eligibility and acceptance evidence may be retained for the period reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance or deal with a claim. Public content remains available until deleted, removed or restricted. A deletion request starts a verified process; signing out, uninstalling and deactivation are different actions.

Verification and reset codes expire after 15 minutes. Expired challenge digests are cleared during later challenge processing. Rate-limit buckets are short-lived and pruned during later requests. Operational and safety records are kept according to the investigation, incident, legal requirement or claim for which they are needed. We retain identifiable measurement records only while reasonably needed to evaluate the pilot or address a data request. Where possible, we use aggregated results afterwards.

After a valid erasure request we delete or anonymize account and associated personal data unless a specific ground requires retention. Restricted backup copies may persist until the normal backup cycle overwrites them. Material needed for a lawful preservation order, safety investigation or legal claim is restricted to that purpose. These criteria also apply to information received from reporters and non-users. Ask us about the period applicable to a particular record.

Security and incidents

We use controls appropriate to the data and risk, including password hashing, expiring single-use email codes and authenticated access to private account functions. The public site and API are intended to be used over HTTPS. Phishing, inbox compromise, software defects, unauthorized access, accidental disclosure and copying of public uploads remain possible. BST is a small team. That fact does not reduce our legal duty to use appropriate safeguards or give us permission to ignore an incident.

Report a suspected compromise to our security contact. Describe what happened and when. Keep passwords, verification codes and harmful media out of your message. We investigate, seek to contain incidents, and notify authorities and affected people when the applicable legal thresholds are met.

Your rights and choices

Under the GDPR where applicable, you may request access and a copy, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of data you provided for automated processing based on consent or contract. You may withdraw optional consent at any time. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation. We must stop unless applicable law permits us to continue for compelling overriding grounds or legal claims. You may object to direct marketing at any time. These rights can have legal limits, including the rights of others. In other regions, you or an authorized agent may exercise applicable local rights or appeal a denied request by contacting us. We will explain our decision. The pilot does not sell personal data or share it with ad networks for cross-site targeted advertising.

Use Account → Settings → Request data or Delete request, or email hello@isnapy.com. If you have no account, identify the post, URL or username concerned. We may ask for proportionate proof of identity. Under the GDPR, we respond without undue delay and normally within one month. If complexity or the number of requests makes an extension necessary, we tell you within the first month and explain why. There is normally no fee.

You may complain to the supervisory authority in the EEA country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. Find the appropriate authority in the European Data Protection Board directory. You may also seek judicial remedies. Where AIFC data protection rules apply, you may contact the AIFC data protection authority. Contacting BST first is optional.

Changes to this notice

The revision and date are shown above. We explain material changes before they take effect where required. A materially different purpose, new tracking or a new AI use may require further notice, assessment or fresh consent. Acknowledging a revised notice does not waive any privacy right.
