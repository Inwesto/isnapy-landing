How clips are ranked and checked

The pilot combines recent videos with clips ranked using signals such as battle power, comments and likes. The homepage can select a pair from the public feed. The game derives character labels and scores from clip metadata, categories, stickers and game events. These labels describe a game character and can be wrong. They should never be treated as findings about a person’s identity, emotions, safety or character.

Automated keyword checks can flag material for review or affect a game score. A keyword match alone cannot establish whether a video is lawful. We also consider reports, surrounding context, applicable law and the rights of those involved. BST does not promise to see every upload before it appears. See the privacy explanation of ranking and optional measurement.
