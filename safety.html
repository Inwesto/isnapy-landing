Report a clip, account or scam

You do not need to be a member or to log in. Email hello@isnapy.com. You may write directly using the instructions below. We accept plain-language reports. English is our working language. If someone faces immediate danger, contact the local emergency services first.

Send a safety notice

Opening a link prepares an email on your device. Check the details, then press Send in your mail app. If no mail app opens, copy our address into your own email service.

Prepare a report ↗
  • Locate it. Give the exact URL or video ID. If the app does not show a link, supply the handle, title, approximate time, and enough detail to identify the item.
  • Explain it. Identify the rule, right or law involved, the country if relevant, and why you believe the item breaches it. Examples include a phishing profile, stolen soundtrack, sexually explicit post or threat of violence.
  • Contact and good faith. For a formal EU illegal-content notice, provide your name, email address and a statement that you believe in good faith that your information is accurate and complete. Name and email are not required elements under the DSA for notices concerning specified child sexual abuse offences. Email may expose the sender address; use a suitable reporting or official channel if confidentiality is critical.

Child safety and intimate imagery

Identify the item without copying, downloading, attaching or forwarding sexual material involving minors. Report suspected exploitation to the appropriate national hotline or police. We can act on the location and a brief description. The INHOPE network can help locate a hotline.

If an intimate image or realistic digital forgery of you was posted without consent, you or an authorized representative may request removal. Provide a description and location sufficient to find it, a way to contact you, a statement that you believe in good faith that the depiction was published without the depicted individual’s consent, any relevant information about its origin, and your physical or electronic signature. State your relationship to the affected person if acting on their behalf. Do not attach the image.

We prioritize these requests. Where the US TAKE IT DOWN Act applies, we remove a qualifying depiction as soon as possible and within 48 hours of receiving a valid request, and make reasonable efforts to identify and remove known identical copies within that time.

Copyright and impersonation

Identify your work or the person impersonated, your authority to act, the relevant item and how to contact you. Explain any relevant permission or lawful exception. We assess competing claims and may request clarification. You can also report a scam without owning copyright.

What happens next

We acknowledge receipt when contact information is available, identify the item, and review the context and applicable rules. We may request more detail, restrict access, preserve evidence or refer a matter to authorities. Where appropriate we explain the outcome and available options to the reporter and affected user. We limit disclosure of your identity where possible, subject to legal duties and fair process. The Privacy Policy describes how we handle reports.

Appeal a decision

If we restricted your content or account, or you believe we wrongly kept reported content available, reply to our decision or ask for human review. Include the decision or report reference, affected item, date and the grounds for reconsideration. For example, explain permission, parody, mistaken identity or missing context. There is no fee. A reviewer can restore material or change a restriction. Other remedies available by law, including applicable out-of-court dispute settlement and access to courts, remain available.

Authorities and security incidents

Authorities may contact BST’s electronic contact point with their authority, jurisdiction, legal basis, specific item, required action, deadline and a verifiable reply channel. Users and authorities may also use the registered office below. For account compromise, a vulnerability or a suspected data breach, use the subject Security incident. Send minimal evidence and ask for a secure handoff before sharing sensitive details.
